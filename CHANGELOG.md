# Changelog

Each release's notes come from its section here (the release workflow
copies the section whose heading matches the tag). Newest first.
Versions: `v1.2.0` is Stable, `v1.3.0-beta.1` is Beta.

## [0.1.0-beta.2]

- **Accounts (new, optional)**: sign in with Google or an emailed link to keep your progress in step across laptops, and add friends by code. Settings → Account. Signed out, the game plays exactly as before.
- **Timing ghosts**: the faint copies showing early and late notes now sit beside the note, so you can see them while practising.

## [0.1.0-beta.1]

The first Headliner build you can install.

- **Career**: a hometown, a guitarist, the tour from open mics to arenas, gigs with a crowd that cheers (and, at tough venues, boos).
- **Battles**: trade phrases with friends on the same laptop, with rivals, your own ghost or the Session Player; records per profile; eight battle riffs.
- **Progress**: levels, XP (more for slow practice), streaks, badges, several profiles.
- **Updates**: Headliner checks for new versions and installs them for you, backing up your saves first. Settings → About & updates.

# Installing Headliner

Headliner is a guitar game that listens to you play a real guitar.

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or 11 laptop or PC.
- An electric guitar.
- A way to get the guitar into the computer: a **USB audio interface** (e.g. a Focusrite Scarlett) or a **USB amp** (e.g. a Marshall CODE, Boss Katana, Fender Mustang).
- Headphones, or the amp's own speaker.

## Install

1. Go to the Releases page: https://github.com/nycc7yfw75-bot/instrumentTutor/releases
2. Under the newest release, click **Headliner-Setup-….exe** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file.
4. Windows may show a blue box: **"Windows protected your PC"**. Click
   **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears because Headliner isn't
   paid-for "signed" software, not because anything is wrong with it.
5. Click through the installer. No admin password is needed. Tick "Start Headliner" at the end.

## First launch

1. Type your name.
2. Pick your hometown and your guitarist.
3. Open **Settings** (the gear, top right). Under AUDIO choose your interface
   or amp for both **input** and **output**.
4. **Tap calibrate**: pluck a string along with the clicks until it's done.
   This lines your playing up with the game's timing; without it, scores
   say "Unscored".
5. Back on the main screen: **Practice** to learn songs, **Perform** for your
   career, **Battles** to play against friends or rivals.

Headliner keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out it asks you
when you open it. Your progress is backed up before every update.

## If something's wrong

- **It doesn't hear me / no input**: Settings → AUDIO → check the input is
  your interface or amp, and its gain knob is up. Play: the level meter
  should move.
- **"Unscored — calibrate"**: do Tap calibrate in Settings (step 4 above).
- **Windows Defender removed it / "virus detected"**: unsigned new apps are
  sometimes flagged by mistake. Open Windows Security → Virus & threat
  protection → Protection history, find Headliner, choose **Restore**. Then
  install again.
- **Uninstalling**: Windows Settings → Apps → Headliner → Uninstall. Your
  progress is kept in `%APPDATA%\headliner` in case you come back.

# HEADLINER — debug session checklist

Everything below was built and saved **without being tried on the guitar**.
Work down the list. For each test:

- Tick the box when it works: change `- [ ]` to `- [x]`.
- If it doesn't work, leave the box empty and write what happened on the **Notes:** line
  (what you clicked, what you expected, what you saw). That's all I need to fix it.
- If you skip one, write "skipped" in Notes.

Last updated: 2026-11-21 (after redesign Stage 4, the start screen).

## 7. Scoring while you play

Use **First Steps** first (fairest), then try Sultans.

- [ ] With calibration done (section 5), open First Steps. The bottom right shows "LV 1 · Bedroom Player" (or your level) and a small XP bar, **not** "Unscored".
  Notes:
- [ ] Play along. Notes you hit get a **filled green square**; close ones a **yellow outline**; missed ones turn **red with a small ×**. The counters and accuracy % at the top right go up.
  Notes:
- [ ] The excitement bar (bottom right) fills as you play well and slowly drains while the song plays. It never jumps down because of a miss.
  Notes:
- [ ] Pause mid-song: the excitement bar stops draining.
  Notes:
- [ ] Turn **Wait** on (**W**). The bottom shows "Unscored — wait mode is practice". Turn it off again.
  Notes:
- [ ] Hit a note early or late (not perfectly). A faint dashed copy of the note (a "ghost") appears where you actually played it, with a ‹ (early) or › (late) mark, and fades away after about 2 bars. Perfect notes and misses get no ghost.
  Notes:
- [ ] **Sultans**: bends, chords, dead notes (x) and grace notes are **never** marked as misses. (Some false misses on fingerstyle/legato notes are expected; write down any that look badly wrong.)
  Notes:
- [ ] Your score never goes down after a miss: misses add nothing, but they never take anything away.
  Notes:

## 8. XP and levels

- [ ] Set a short loop (A … B, about 4 bars) in First Steps and play it through. When the loop goes round, a "+N XP" message appears and the XP bar grows.
  Notes:
- [ ] Play the same loop 6 times in a row. The XP per pass gets smaller each time but never drops to zero.
  Notes:
- [ ] **Slow practice earns more.** Play the same loop at 100% and at 50% (press ↓ ten times). At 50% the "+N XP" is bigger, up to 1.5× (for example +36 instead of +27; the flat 10 XP per pass doesn't grow). At 75% it's about 1.25×. Going faster than 100% earns no extra.
  Notes:
- [ ] Pausing also ends a pass: after pausing, any playing since the last start (8 notes or more) gives its XP.
  Notes:
- [ ] If you level up, a level card appears **after** the pass, never in the middle of playing.
  Notes:
- [ ] Close the app and reopen it. Your level and XP are the same as before.
  Notes:
- [ ] In `C:\Users\Dell\AppData\Roaming\musictutor\` you see `progress.json` and backup copies `progress.json.1`, `.2`, `.3`.
  Notes:

## 8b. Practice streak

A day counts when the song has been **playing** for 5 minutes or more that day. Scored or unscored, listening or not, it all counts. Paused time doesn't.

- [ ] Day 1: play any song for 5+ minutes in total (it can be several short goes). Stop playback. The bottom-right line now says "LV … · 1-day streak". Settings → PROFILE shows it too.
  Notes:
- [ ] It counts with listening off too: play for 5 minutes with the guitar input not chosen ("Not listening" showing). The day still counts.
  Notes:
- [ ] Day 2, before playing: the line says "1-day streak, 5 min to go today". After 5 minutes of playing it changes to "2-day streak".
  Notes:
- [ ] Skip a whole day, then play: the streak starts again from 1. Nothing else is taken away (level, XP, bests stay).
  Notes:
